# Problem statement

Fuel consumption is a major operational cost and emissions driver in shipping. Vessel fuel demand varies with speed, environmental conditions and operating context, while conventional speed-only relationships cannot represent all observed variability. This project develops and prospectively evaluates a reproducible machine-learning decision-support prototype for an anonymised cruise passenger vessel. It predicts total momentary fuel consumption from variables available at scenario time, compares the prediction against a physically motivated cubic speed baseline, and evaluates alternative constant ground speeds subject to voyage-time and empirical-support constraints. The system is an exploratory engineering tool rather than a certified voyage planner; predicted savings require independent operational validation.
